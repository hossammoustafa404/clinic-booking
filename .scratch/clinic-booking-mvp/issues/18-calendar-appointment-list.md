# 18: Calendar and Appointment list

**What to build:** Staff use a day and week calendar starting Saturday and a paginated Appointment list, with filters and a detail panel.

**Blocked by:** 06 — Working Hours and Time Off, 17 — Staff Booking

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The week starts Saturday; Friday and Saturday are shaded by default.
- [ ] The calendar shows Working Hours, Time Off, Appointment blocks, and buffer shading.
- [ ] Filters cover Practitioner, Service, and status.
- [ ] The Appointment detail panel opens from the calendar and the list.
- [ ] The list is paginated and searchable by Patient name or phone.
- [ ] Mobile shows an agenda list instead of a compressed grid.
- [ ] A Practitioner sees their own schedule by default.
- [ ] Playwright covers the calendar and list in both locales.
